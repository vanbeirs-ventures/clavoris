Company accounts

The companies that use Clavoris for data rooms, contract signature and private investments. Each data room, product and contract belongs to one company; an admin user is attached to one company and sees only its data.

Data rooms

Invite by email address with an access end date: the user is enrolled if new, receives an invitation naming your company and the end date, sets a passphrase, completes KYC, signs the room's NDA and gets access. Documents are read from the room's Nextcloud folder. Reminders go out 7 days and 24 hours before the end date; after it, the member can still open the room and download their signed NDA, nothing else.

File notifications

Every 15 minutes Clavoris compares each room's folder with its last snapshot and mails the members about new and updated files (unless they switched the notification off under Account). The first scan of a room only records the files already there.
